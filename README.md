privacy policy for lutrin, an app on google play

App Name: Lutrin 
Developer / Organization: The Improbable Codelab (com.theimprobablecodelab.lutrin)

1. Introduction
Welcome to Lutrin ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (Lutrin).
Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide better services to our users. Depending on how you interact with Lutrin, we may collect the following types of information:

• Account & Authentication Information: If you sign in using Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address, authentication credentials, and account identifier to manage your account and sync your data securely.

• User Content & App Data: Information and tasks you create within the app are stored locally on your device (via Room Database and DataStore) and may be synced to cloud storage (Firebase Firestore) if you are signed into an account.

• Analytics & Usage Data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, app performance data, crash reports, and interaction metrics to help us improve the app experience.


3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

• To create, maintain, and secure your user account.

• To provide, operate, and maintain the core functionality of Lutrin (such as task management and data sync).

• To improve user experience through analytics and crash diagnostics.

• To serve advertisements via Google Mobile Ads.

• To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Third-Party Services
Lutrin integrates with trusted third-party services that may collect data governed by their respective privacy policies:

• Firebase (Google): Used for authentication, cloud database storage, cloud functions, and analytics. 


5. Data Security
We implement reasonable security measures (including encryption in transit via HTTPS/TLS and secure cloud storage rules) to protect your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage technology is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

• The app does not have an account system, so there is nothing to delete.
• There is no data retention.

7. Children's Privacy
Lutrin is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or higher depending on local jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date.
